Coffee Shop Management: The Operations Guide

The day-to-day of running a coffee shop is where margin is quietly won or lost: in the schedule, the ordering, the portioning, and the numbers you do or don't read each week. This guide pulls together the operational side of the business into one place.

Second Manager · Guide

Great coffee gets people in the door; operations decide whether you keep any money once they're there. Your schedule sets your labor and payroll percentage, your ordering and portioning set your waste, and your weekly read of the numbers, including your sales per hour, decides whether you catch problems early or late.
